Why flat?

Credits are a tax on planning.

You shouldn't have to do mental math at 2am to know whether tomorrow's article will publish. With Bylined, your December bill is knowable in May.

Credit pricing
"1,500 credits" → an article costs "between 30 and 80 credits depending on length, model, and SERP depth." Run out mid-month, choose between paying overage at 2× the per-article rate or pausing the publishing schedule.
Bylined pricing
"Studio is 30 articles a month for $99." If you publish 31, the 31st costs $4. The number on the invoice is the number you expected, plus or minus a few dollars in overage.
Pricing FAQ

Common questions.

What if I publish more articles than my plan?
Overage rate is published per tier (Solo $5, Studio $4, Agency $3, Scale $2 per extra article). You set a hard cap in Billing if you want to pause instead.
Can I switch tiers mid-month?
Yes. We pro-rate up. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle so you keep what you paid for.
Do you offer a non-profit / academic discount?
50% off any tier with verifiable status. Email founders@bylined.so.
Is there an annual contract for Scale?
Optional. We default to month-to-month even on Scale; annual gets you 20% off and a guarantee on the volume cap.
What about taxes?
Sales tax / VAT is calculated at checkout based on your billing address. EU customers can supply a VAT ID.
